# Ownership and hands-off rules

Constraints Meio has stated. The PM copies the relevant lines into every brief.

- Accepted scooter v012 and the archived character originals: read-only. Work on copies.
- Gold baselines: never changed to fit a model. Only Meio approves new or corrected gold.
- Blender scenes: the 3D Artist only, one operator per scene.
- Runtime code may not reshape or re-pose assets (ADR 0001). Asset fixes go back to the 3D Artist.
- Gameplay physics and colliders: preserved unless Meio approves a change.
- Promotion of Ride Lab into the homepage, and deploys: separate decisions by Meio.
